HUMAN RESOURCES
You read three hundred CVs for three positions
Screening happens in a rush, by a different person each time, against criteria written down nowhere. Good applications get lost, and candidates wait three weeks for an answer.
An agent that ranks applications against criteria you have written down and made explicit, prepares an interview grid for each position, and decides nothing: it orders, you decide. CV screening is a high-risk use under the AI Act — the criteria are documented, deviations are logged, and no rejection is automatic.
