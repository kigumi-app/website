MANUFACTURING AND B2B DISTRIBUTION
Your orders arrive as PDFs. They leave as re-keying.
One order in two arrives by email, as an attachment, sometimes scanned. Someone reads it, translates it, checks it, then keys it in again.
The situation
Someone finds the customer, translates their references into your catalogue, checks prices and lead times, then re-keys everything into the ERP. That translation between your customers’ language and your actual references is written down nowhere: it lives in the head of your sales administration team. The day that person is away, everything slows down.
What we put in place
An agent that reads the order, identifies the customer and their commercial terms, matches the references, checks consistency — prices, quantities, lead times, credit limits — prepares the entry and sends the acknowledgement.
Anything it isn’t sure about goes to your sales administration team, with the reason for the doubt. And the mapping that existed only in one person’s head becomes a company asset.
