LAB
What we build outside our missions.
Our method rests on one promise: we measure what agents produce, instead of noting that they run. You still have to know how to do it. That’s what we build here.
Why it isn’t a side project
Software that runs is not software that does what was expected of it. We can say that a program doesn’t crash; we can rarely say what it does. That gap was already hard when humans wrote the code. It becomes the central problem now that part of it is produced by models.
It’s also what separates an automation that pays from one that costs. The work we do here isn’t a product we sell you: it’s the reason why, for our customers, we measure instead of assuming.
