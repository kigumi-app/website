Your data, where it goes, and what you keep.
The questions your IT department will ask anyway. Might as well answer them before the call.
Hosting
In France, with Scaleway and OVHcloud, two hosting providers incorporated under French law. One isolated database per customer, no pooling of data between our customers.
What leaves, what doesn’t
Only what the requested processing strictly needs, and nothing else. No model training on your data.
The model providers we use, and the country where processing takes place:
Mistral AI — France
Anthropic — United States
OpenAI — United States
Google — United States
TypeSafe AI — United States
The choice is made process by process, with you. When processing has to stay in Europe, it stays in Europe.
How long your data is kept
Documents processed by the agents — 30 days after processing. Enough to correct an error, no more.
Knowledge base — the length of the contract. Returned to you, then deleted within 30 days if you stop.
Technical logs — 6 months, the period the CNIL (the French data protection authority) sets for connection logs.
Data sent to model providers — zero retention where the provider’s offering allows it. Otherwise, 30 days for abuse detection only. In every case, no use for training.
Your contact details, if you have contacted us — 3 years after the last exchange.
Who has access, and to what
Named accounts, never shared, with two-factor authentication on anything touching your data.
Least privilege: each person only accesses the customers they work with.
No standing access to production. Access is opened on request, for a limited time, and it is logged.
Access reviewed every quarter. Revoked within 24 hours when someone leaves or a mission ends.
What is logged
Who accessed what, when, and what was done. Timestamped, tamper-proof.
Every action of an agent is logged — the data read, the decision, the output produced, and the point of human approval where there is one.
Logs kept for 6 months.
Available to you, on request. A log the customer can’t read proves nothing.
The AI Act requires this traceability for high-risk systems. We apply it to every agent we deploy, even when nothing requires it: that’s what makes a case like CV screening deployable instead of risky.
Reversibility
The extracted data and the process definitions belong to you, and can be exported at any time. If you stop, you keep what was built.
Humans stay in control
Any irreversible step, or one that carries a commercial commitment, is approved by someone on your team. An agent proposes, flags what it isn’t sure about, and never commits the company on your behalf.

AI Act
The European regulation on artificial intelligence classifies uses by level of risk. The automations we deploy — document processing, request routing, drafting assistance with human approval — fall under limited risk, with a transparency obligation.
Two cases call for particular attention, and we point them out before we start: when the people interacting with an agent are consumers, they must be told; and any automation involving the assessment of people, recruitment in particular, is high-risk and carries much heavier obligations.
