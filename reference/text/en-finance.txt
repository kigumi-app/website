FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION
Your invoices go out, payments lag behind
Reminders go out when someone thinks of them. Payments are matched by hand, and disputes wait for someone to investigate them.
An agent that sends reminders based on each customer’s history rather than the calendar, matches incoming payments, and prepares each dispute with the documents that relate to it.
WHAT WE MEASURE
Days sales outstanding (DSO)
Reminders per payment collected
Cost of disputes left uninvestigated
FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION
Your supplier invoices and expense claims are keyed in by hand
Every document is read, coded, matched to a purchase order, then entered. The volume is constant and the added value is zero.
An agent that reads the document, matches it to the corresponding order or contract, and prepares the entry — escalating anything that doesn’t match.
WHAT WE MEASURE
Processing cost per document
Time to month-end close
FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION
Your contracts renew without you noticing
Expiry dates, indexation, automatic renewals. The dates are in the contracts, the contracts are in a folder, and nobody rereads them.
An agent that tracks the expiry dates and revision clauses of your contracts, and alerts you within the window where you can still renegotiate.
WHAT WE MEASURE
Renewals you didn’t choose
Gap to the terms that could have been renegotiated
FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION
Your budget variances get explained after the fact
The variance shows up at close. Understanding where it comes from takes a week, and the reforecast arrives when the decision it was meant to inform has already been made.
An agent that reconciles actuals against budget as you go, isolates the lines that explain the variance, and escalates those outside your thresholds — with the detail that lets you check rather than take it on trust.
WHAT WE MEASURE
Time to explain a variance
Reforecasts per year
Share of variances explained before close
FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION
Your cash position is a day old
Several banks, sometimes several currencies. Consolidation is done by hand in a spreadsheet, and payment peaks are discovered the day they hit.
An agent that consolidates the positions, matches movements to known invoices and due dates, and alerts you to upcoming payments that won’t clear — early enough to act.
WHAT WE MEASURE
Time to consolidate across banks
Unanticipated overdrafts
Gap between forecast and actual position
