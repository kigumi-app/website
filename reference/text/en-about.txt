PORTRAIT COMING SOON
Julien
Operations and business development.
I started with seven years selling IT solutions to large accounts, for telecom operators and integrators: calls for tender, IT departments, procurement, coordination between pre-sales, manufacturers and project teams.
I then spent six years at a mobility software publisher, where I went from Customer Success Manager to Client Services Director, leading a team of 11 across Customer Success, Support and project management.
My job for nearly fifteen years: making sure a technical solution keeps its promises once it is at the customer’s.
PORTRAIT COMING SOON
Stéphane
Technology and product.
Nearly fifteen years building complex software products used by millions of people — where a mistake shows immediately.
I ran all the development teams of a mobility software publisher, R&D and machine learning included.
What brought me here is what I saw happen again and again: a system that runs is not a system that does what was expected of it. We can say that software doesn’t crash; we can rarely say what it does. I have been working on verification ever since — how to make sure a program, and today a program written by an AI, does what it was asked to do.
It’s the same standard I apply to the agents we deploy: we don’t check that they run, we measure what they produce.
Why the two of us
We worked together for three years at the same mobility software publisher. Julien ran customer relations — customer success, support, complex projects. Stéphane ran all the development teams, R&D and machine learning included. One took the calls when the product didn’t do what it was said to do; the other led the teams that had built it.
We each came to the same conclusion: what is rarely missing is the ability to build. What is almost always missing is proof of what was built.
That’s why we don’t sell pilots: we cost first, deploy next, and measure what it produced. And that’s why we work with companies your size — in a large group, an automation that doesn’t hold gets diluted, there is always someone to catch it by hand. At your company, it shows immediately.
The two people who sell you the mission are the two who deliver it.
