CONSULTING, ENGINEERING AND DESIGN OFFICES
You lose tenders for lack of time, not for lack of arguments.
Every tender ties up three or four people for several days. And some tenders never go out, simply because there wasn’t time.
The situation
The answers already exist. They are in your past tenders, your references, your CVs, your certifications, your methodology documents. With every new questionnaire — call for tender, procurement questionnaire, security or CSR questionnaire — everything is rewritten from the start.
What we put in place
An agent that reads the tender whatever its format, extracts the questions, and finds what you have already answered in comparable tenders. It proposes a draft for each question, with its source and its confidence level, and flags new questions separately — the ones that deserve your decision.
You reread, you correct, you approve. The agent never submits anything on your behalf.
