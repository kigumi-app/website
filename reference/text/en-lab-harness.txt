Harness
LAB · INTERNAL TOOL
Know what a system does, and be able to prove it.
A system that runs is not a system that does what was expected. Harness is the tool we are building to tell the difference: it reads a process, maps it, and verifies that every expected behaviour holds.
OVERVIEW
HOW IT WORKS
Why we are building it
Our method promises to measure what agents produce, instead of noting that they run. That promise only holds if we can say, at any moment, what an automated process actually does: which rules it applies, which exceptions it escalates, what it does when a piece of data is missing.
We can say that a program doesn’t crash. We can rarely say what it does. Harness closes that gap, for our own deliveries first.
What it does
SEE
The map of the process
Every step, every rule, every exception, and what connects them. Drawn from what actually runs, not from what the procedure says.
CHANGE
What a change affects
Before changing a rule, you see what it touches. The change is proposed, then reviewed by someone before it goes out.
VERIFY
What holds, and what doesn’t
Every expected behaviour is verified. Gaps are named, with where they occur, before they cost anything.
