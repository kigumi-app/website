# Kigumi — Marketing Site Context

register: brand

## Users

**Primary visitor:** CTOs, technical founders, and senior engineers at companies with 5–50 engineers. They arrive from LinkedIn, conference talks, or word-of-mouth. They are skeptical, technically literate, and allergic to hype. They read the page to understand what Kigumi is, whether it applies to their situation, and whether it is worth a demo.

**Secondary visitor:** VCs and angels at pre-seed/seed stage, evaluating the team and market thesis.

## Product Purpose

Kigumi is a behavior comprehension platform. It maps what a codebase *does* — decisions, rules, and flows — into a typed, verified graph. The marketing site's job is to make that claim credible in 30 seconds, then earn the next 3 minutes.

The tone is confident and precise, not persuasive. The reader is smart; treat them that way. No "unlock the power of" language. No "AI-powered" as a standalone claim. Every sentence earns its place.

## Brand Personality

**Creative North Star: "Workshop at Night"**

A craftsman's workshop at 2am. Warm wood tones, blueprint precision, lamplight focus. The surface is intentionally dark and warm — dark because this is a tool for focused technical work, warm because it is built with craft, not just utility.

Fraunces is the headline voice: a variable optical-size serif with rounded warmth at large sizes. It reads like lamplight on paper — considered, precise, unhurried. Inter handles body and UI. The contrast between the two creates the workshop atmosphere: a craftsman's hand for titles, a technical eye for details.

Amber is the single accent. It appears where something matters: CTAs, active states, highlights. It does not appear as decoration.

**Tone:** Warm confidence. Direct. No hedging. A practitioner talking to other practitioners.

**What this is not:** A startup vanity page. Generic SaaS. "AI-powered" hype. Cold corporate.

## Anti-References

- **Generic SaaS gradients** — purple-to-teal, teal-to-blue. No gradient as personality.
- **Cold dark mode** — dark with blue-white text and no warmth. The chill of a hospital.
- **Neon accents** — electric green, hot pink, vivid cyan. Wrong temperature.
- **Flat tech blue** — the safe default. Not here.
- **Light mode** — this site is dark-only.
- **Stock photo humans** — no smiling people in offices. No hands on keyboards. No abstract tech swirls.
- **Buzzword density** — "next-generation", "unlock", "empower", "game-changer". Prohibited.
- **Dashboard energy** — big KPI numbers, chart previews everywhere. This is a comprehension tool, not an analytics dashboard.

## Strategic Principles

1. **Credibility over conversion.** The reader is technical. Trust is built by being precise, not by being enthusiastic.
2. **One accent, one job.** Amber appears on CTAs and key highlights only. If it appears everywhere, it means nothing.
3. **Texture is atmosphere.** Film grain, vignette, and the top light line create the Workshop at Night feel. They are structural, not decorative.
4. **Fraunces carries the brand voice.** At large optical sizes with SOFT 70, it is warm and distinctive. Use it for hero headlines and section titles only — never at body size.
5. **The graph is the proof.** Product screenshots and graph visualizations are the strongest trust signals. Put them in view early.
